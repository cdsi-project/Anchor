# CDSI Anchor Brand Assets

## Concept

The Anchor mark is one continuous path that enters through an open corner and
terminates at a green origin point:

- the continuous path represents one installation script;
- the open boundary represents an independently owned OpenWeb;
- the green square represents the creator's digital origin.

The mark deliberately avoids a literal maritime anchor. It is intended to feel
like infrastructure: simple, stable, inspectable, and replaceable.

## Usage

- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the width of its center square.
- Use the standalone mark below 280 px wide; use the lockup at larger sizes.
- Do not rotate the mark, close its open corner, add shadows, or recolor its
  center independently from the approved palette.
- Keep the tagline separate from the compact mark.

Tagline:

> 个人数字主权，从一个脚本开始。
